How It Works

1. Right-Click and Draw

Hold right-click (mouse) or use trackpad. Draw your gesture freely. Release to trigger.

2. Map to Actions

Assign gestures to shortcuts, app switches, or custom commands. Per-app settings for precision.

3. Refine and Manage

Batch enable/disable flows. View start/end markers while drawing. Updates notify from menu bar.

4. Use Everywhere

Works across apps. Stacks with macOS gestures. Instant response, no overhead.
